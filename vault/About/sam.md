# Sam Okafor — Operations Manager

*(Fictional worked example.)*

Sam runs the machinery: renewals, invoicing, venues, the member records, the board pack.

## Named recurring jobs

- Renewals correspondence (May–July peak) and invoice chasing.
- Board, committee and AGM pack assembly — **board material is in-confidence and stays
  out of this vault and out of every retrieval pass**.
- Member-data hygiene in the CRM.

## Working with Sam

- Sam asks precise, closed questions and expects every one answered — a reply that
  covers two of three comes straight back.
- Never estimate a date or an amount. Venues, budgets and invoices are contractual —
  find the number or say you can't.
- The accounting package is Sam's. Guide; Sam clicks — no actions with financial effect.
