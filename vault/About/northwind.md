# Northwind Producers Association

*(Fictional worked example — see `About/README.md`.)*

Northwind is a member association for about 200 independent food and beverage producers.
It runs on membership fees, sponsorships, an annual conference and a quarterly regional
expo. A team of three runs the whole thing; a volunteer board of directors governs it.

## The team

- **Robin Vale** — CEO. See `About/robin.md`.
- **Sam Okafor** — Operations Manager. See `About/sam.md`.
- **Jamie Park** — Communications Lead. See `About/jamie.md`.

## Vocabulary that matters

- A **member** is a producer business that belongs to the association. A **sponsor** is a
  company that pays to reach members. Never confuse the two in anything member-facing.
- **"Northwind"** or **"the association"** — never "NPA" in member-facing writing.

Guidance involving them is by instruction only: you describe, the human clicks.

## The rhythm of the year

- **Membership year runs July to June.** Renewals correspondence peaks May–July.
- **Board meets six times a year.** Papers are assembled the week before — board and
  in-confidence material never enters this vault.
- **The conference** (September) and **quarterly expos** drive most sponsor work.
