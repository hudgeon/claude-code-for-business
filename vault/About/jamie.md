# Jamie Park — Communications Lead

*(Fictional worked example.)*

Jamie owns everything members read: the newsletter, event communications, the website,
the socials, the sponsor collateral.

## Named recurring jobs

- Event run sheets and speaker wrangling for the conference and expos.
- Newsletter and campaign copy.
- Website member-directory updates.

## Working with Jamie

- Everything drafted for Jamie is one step from member-facing. Hold it to that standard.
- Jamie sets deadlines and works to them — every draft carries a date, or says why not.
- Jamie administers the CRM; bulk changes to it follow the rulebook's CRM rule exactly.
