# Robin Vale — CEO

*(Fictional worked example.)*

Robin owns the relationships: members, sponsors, the board, the industry bodies. Most of
what Robin does starts or ends as an email.

## Named recurring jobs

- Sponsorship approaches and renewals — the largest repeating workload.
- Board paper narrative (the covering report; Sam assembles the pack).
- Partnership and membership decisions — always drafted, never delegated.

## Working with Robin

- Robin's inbox is Robin's task system. Flagged mail is the live queue, not the task app.
- Drafts over summaries: Robin would rather edit a ready draft than read a briefing.
- Anything touching a sponsor's money or a member's standing goes to Robin before it is
  written down anywhere shared.
